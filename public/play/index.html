A LITTLE GUIDE TO LEELA

How to play

  1. Roll & travel. Dice chalao. Turn par ek optional power card use kar sakte ho, roll se pehle.
  2. Collect 3 matkis. Golden stops 19, 44, 69 aur 89 se guzarte hi matki milti hai. Ek stop se ek matki per lap.
  3. Choose a route. Junction par normal trail se matki lo, ya dotted shortcut se aage niklo.
  4. Play a card. Flute: +2 moves. Feather: +1 matki. Shield: next hazard se bachao. Har turn sirf ek card.
  5. Friendly monkey. Monkey par land hue toh 4 steps peeche! Blue slides neeche le jaati hain; peacock paths aage. Shield ek hazard rokta hai.
  6. Reach home. 3 matki lekar HOME 100 cross karo aur jeeto! Exact roll zaroori nahi. Kam matki ho toh next lap.

Blue sparkle square par land karo, random card pao. Maximum 3 cards in hand. Sabke paas shuru mein ek Flute, Feather aur Shield hai.
